# Visual identity brainstorm

Before designing a logo and mascot, we need a clear picture of
what torchsnap *is* visually and what it *feels like* to use.

## Name analysis: "Torchsnap"

Two concepts fused together:
- **Torch**: Light, illumination, finding things in the dark,
  guiding the way, a flame, warmth, exploration
- **Snap**: Speed, immediacy, a finger snap, instant, crisp,
  decisive action

The tagline reinforces this: "Light. Find. Launch." — and
"Strike a key, light the way."

## Visual metaphors to explore

### Torch / flame
- A stylized torch or flame as the primary icon
- Flame as a cursor/pointer — the flame IS the search
- A matchstick striking — the "snap" moment of ignition
- A lantern illuminating a dark space

### Snap / speed
- Lightning bolt or spark
- A finger snap with a flash of light
- A camera-style snap (aperture + flash)
- Motion lines, streak of light

### Combined concepts
- A flame that forms a magnifying glass shape
- A spark/flash at the moment of a finger snap
- A torch beam that highlights/selects items
- A matchstick head as the app icon, flame as the active state

### Mascot possibilities
- A firefly / lightning bug — small, quick, illuminating, friendly
- A fox with a torch (à la Firefox, but different)
- An owl with glowing eyes (wisdom + seeing in the dark)
- A dragon breathing a focused beam of fire
- Abstract: a friendly flame character (like Calcifer from Howl's
  Moving Castle)
- A lighthouse keeper character

## Visual style direction

### Launcher bar aesthetic
- The bar itself should feel like a spotlight beam or torch glow
  on a dark surface
- Subtle warm glow/gradient around the card edges?
- The accent color (currently orange) fits the flame/torch theme
  — warm, energetic, stands out on dark backgrounds
- Consider a subtle radial gradient behind the card in dark mode
  (like light emanating from the search bar)

### Overall feel
- **Warm but minimal**: The fire/torch theme could easily become
  heavy or medieval. Keep it modern and clean.
- **Fast and precise**: Animations should be quick and snappy,
  not floaty. The "snap" in the name.
- **Confident**: Strong accent color, clear typography, decisive
  interactions.

## Questions to resolve

- Mascot or no mascot? Squirly has one and it adds personality,
  but not every app needs one.
- If mascot: should it appear in the launcher bar itself (like
  squirly does) or only in marketing/about screens?
- Icon style: flat, semi-flat with subtle gradients, or 3D?
  macOS favors more detailed icons, but the tray icon needs to
  work as a monochrome template.
- Color palette: stick with orange as brand primary (matches
  torch/flame theme well) or explore alternatives?
